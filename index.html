Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you begin.

Does the Mining Career Kit guarantee me a job?

No. The kit is an educational career guide. It helps you understand roles and prepare your research, but employment depends on vacancies, employer criteria, qualifications, experience, work authorisation and each applicant's circumstances.

Does the kit provide an accredited mining qualification?

No. It is an independent information resource, not an accredited course, certificate, licence or formal training programme. Always confirm official qualification and training requirements with the relevant institution or authority.

Do I need a university degree to work in mining?

Not every mining occupation follows the same route. Requirements vary by role and employer; some positions require degrees, while others may follow occupational, technical, artisan or workplace-training pathways. Check the current requirements for the specific role you are considering.

Is the kit only about underground mining?

No. It introduces six areas: underground mining, heavy machinery operation, diesel mechanics, mining engineering, mineral processing, and health and safety.

Will it show me where to find real vacancies?

The kit points you towards a more organised job-search process. Use official employer career pages and follow the application instructions on each vacancy. Never pay an individual who promises to secure a mining job or interview for you.

Is this an official product of a mining company or the MQA?

No. The mYo Mining Career Kit is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or issued by the Mining Qualifications Authority, SAQA or any mining employer.
